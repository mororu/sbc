- (event) Discovery dump: neighbours converted an old worksite container on wheels into a sauna wagon
- (decision) Sited at the public lake-shore swimming place for the winter season; mobile/relocatable asset
- (decision) Open to the public - anyone should be able to book a slot, not just the neighbours
- (decision) Mobile phone is the primary booking surface; must be easy on a small screen
- (decision) Payment is explicitly OUT of scope for the calendar - handled outside the system
- (decision) Admin portal required: block slots for cleaning, maintenance, and private reservations
- (decision) Admin portal required: analytics over bookings and usage
- (assumption) Booking duration/slot length is undecided - open question flagged in discovery
- (event) Stakes read as neighbourhood/community project - brief right-sized accordingly (to confirm)
- (decision) Working mode: Coaching path - section by section, pushback on thin assumptions
- (event) Three load-bearing unknowns raised: (1) no friction - public+free means no cost to book or no-show, (2) booking unit - exclusive whole-wagon vs per-seat shared session, (3) heating - who lights the fire and how that constrains slot structure
- (decision) Wood-fired oven (not electric) - no timer-based remote heating possible
- (decision) Bookers light the fire themselves - no warden or heating rota
- (decision) Minimum slot length is 60 minutes
- (event) Tension surfaced: a cold wood sauna needs ~60-90min to reach temperature, so a 60-min cold-start slot is mostly heating, not sauna - first booker of the day subsidises everyone after them
- (event) Reference comparable supplied by user: seesaunabaldegg.ch - wood-fired sauna wagon on Baldeggersee shore, same concept
- (event) Baldegg cold-start solution: if the preceding slot was unbooked, guests may arrive 30-40 min early to fire the stove at no extra charge - this is the answer to the first-booker penalty and it requires the calendar to expose preceding-slot state
- (event) Baldegg booking unit: exclusive/private use of the whole wagon (zur alleinigen Nutzung), up to 6 people per session
- (event) Baldegg durations: 1h/1.5h/2h/2.5h/3h at CHF 35/50/60/75/90 - 60min minimum in 30min increments, capped at 3h
- (event) Baldegg access: keybox: code plus instructions emailed 24h before the slot; key returned after
- (event) Baldegg payment: on-site via Twint or cash, never in the booking system - matches the payment-out-of-scope constraint
- (event) Baldegg runs on SuperSaas (off-the-shelf); season ends 10 March
- (decision) The sauna is FREE - no charge at all, unlike Baldegg (CHF 35/h). Nothing to collect, on-site or otherwise
- (decision) Core differentiators confirmed: (1) hot/cold intelligence computed per slot, (2) booking and usage analytics in the admin portal
- (decision) Per-person booking cap (configurable max slots) is explicitly FUTURE scope, not v1
- (assumption) Deferring the booking cap still constrains v1: limiting bookings per person later requires capturing identity per booking from the start
- (decision) Analytics scope: all four decision questions adopted - cleaning scheduling, wood/cold-start consumption, season-worth-repeating, and cold-start-driven vacancy
- (decision) Booking captures name, email and mobile phone number
- (decision) Capacity: 6 people per session, exclusive private use of the wagon
- (decision) Access: keybox with an emailed access code (Baldegg model)
- (decision) Season runs October to April - longer than Baldegg, which ends 10 March
- (event) Verification question raised by user (send a token?) - answered: the emailed keybox code is itself the proof of a working email, so v1 needs no separate token flow; explicit verification becomes necessary only when the per-person cap arrives
- (change) Drafted brief.md sections: Executive Summary, The Problem, The Solution, Who This Serves, Analytics
- (override) REVERSAL of the earlier 'sauna is free' decision: the brief must NOT claim free of charge. It states only that payment is handled outside the app. Whether and what guests pay is deliberately left out of scope
- (decision) Languages: German is the default, English also required - across the app, emails and all guest-facing text. i18n is a v1 requirement, not a later addition
- (decision) Duration: 1h to 3h in 30-minute steps (same ladder as Baldegg)
- (decision) Booking horizon: configurable, default 2 weeks ahead
- (decision) V1 cancellation is manual: the guest emails the admins, an admin deletes the slot, and the admin informs affected downstream guests by hand. No guest self-service cancel in v1
- (decision) Admin access: one shared account with a shared password for the neighbour-operators
- (assumption) Manual cancellation implies the admin UI must surface WHO to contact - deleting a slot can flip a later booking from hot start to cold start, so the admin needs that booking's name, email and phone to hand
- (change) Rewrote brief.md: removed all free-of-charge claims, added Scope (in/out) and Success Criteria, added German/English requirement
- (override) REVERSAL: automatic heat-state change notification moves from OUT of v1 to IN v1, at the user's request
- (decision) Heat-state change emails: hot-to-cold always sends (urgent - guest must now arrive early and light the fire); cold-to-hot is good news and lower priority
- (decision) Anti-flapping: notify only when the state differs from what that guest was last told, and settle before sending
- (decision) 24h cliff: beyond 24h the app emails automatically; inside 24h email is too slow, so the admin portal flags it and an admin phones the guest - this is the stated purpose of collecting the mobile number
- (decision) The 24h keybox email carries the authoritative heat state at send time; the booking confirmation states only how things stand at booking
- (assumption) Blast radius of any change is exactly one booking - the slot immediately following the changed one - because hot depends only on the immediately preceding slot
- (change) brief.md updated: heat-state change notification moved into Solution and Scope; removed from the out-of-scope list
- (change) Fixed contradiction: cancellation bullet claimed admins notify affected guests by hand, but notification is now automatic beyond 24h and a phone call only inside 24h
- (decision) Heat rule CONFIRMED: hot only if the immediately preceding slot was taken; gaps do not count. Conservative by design - may tell a guest cold start when the wagon is still warm, never the reverse
- (decision) Pre-heat window confirmed at 30-40 minutes, same as Baldegg
- (decision) Success criterion 'the fire gets lit' is KEPT as an honest statement of what matters, explicitly acknowledged as not measurable by the app
