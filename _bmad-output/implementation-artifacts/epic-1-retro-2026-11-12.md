### Architecture delta

- **The lint-enforced layers hold.** There are no violations and no cycles (`eslint.config.js`; import graph checked across `src/`).
- **Drift: a view-model layer the spine does not name.**
  - `src/lib/server/day-view.ts`, `book-view.ts` and `booked-view.ts`, plus `clock.ts` and `ids.ts`, are not in the layer table (spine L28-38).
  - The view models use `#lib/time` for computation (query windows, `parseStart`, the keybox cutoff), not only for display. So "routes use time for display only" holds only formally.
  - Disposition: reconcile the spine (S1).
- **Rules computed outside the core (AD-1):**
  - The 24 h keybox cutoff lives in `booked-view.ts:25,86-96`; Epic 2's outbox needs the same value.
  - `unavailableNote` re-derives the limits of `fits` (`book-view.ts:121-176` vs `domain/start-times.ts:61-75`).
  - Disposition: fix the keybox cutoff before Epic 2 (A7). Accept the note derivation for now.
- **`day-view.ts` is a hub.** It is imported by `book-view.ts`, `booked-view.ts`, two routes and four components. Disposition: see the size view below.

### Duplication map

- **The day-data window and loading are repeated three times:** `day-view.ts:450`, `book-view.ts:246`, `create-booking.ts:126`. Fold into one helper (A7).
- **The admin contact is read twice with different rules.** See F3.
- **`/day/{date}` URLs are built in 7 places** (`day-view.ts`, `book-view.ts`, `booked-view.ts`), and `'/info'` is hard-coded in `booked-view.ts:163`. Accept for now.
- **The `live()` filter is redundant**, since `getDayItems` already excludes deleted rows (`queries/calendar.ts:50`): `day-view.ts:238`, `book-view.ts:131,148`. Accept (harmless).
- **Test settings literals and the mocked env object are copied** into 7 and 4 test files. Defer: a shared test fixture module (A10).

### Size growth

- **`day-view.ts`** grew 276 → 373 → 392 → 463 lines across Stories 1.4 → 1.5 → 1.6 → 1.7 and now holds about 9 concerns: types, URL builders, error query, copy assembly, free rows, taken intervals, strip and navigation, season view, loader. Split before Epic 3 adds more (A7).

- **Loader return shapes differ:** `{kind}` for the day and book pages, `null` for the confirmation. The booked page keeps its markup inline, while the others use a component. Accept.
- **Route tests depend on the dev-only `DEV_NOW` and on mocking `env.ts`.** Accept.
- **`eslint-disable` comments:** all have inline reasons, except the `svelte-ignore` in `InlineMessage.svelte:24`, whose reason is in the header comment. Accept.
- **Hand-edited migrations** (0000–0003) are documented. The 0003 trigger is not in `meta/0003_snapshot.json`, so regenerating would drop it. Accept; README warns.

## Behavior verification

The guest flows were run end to end on a dev server, against a scratch database with `DEV_NOW=2027-01-12T09:00:00Z` and fixtures, at 360 px. Logs are in the session scratchpad. All 8 checks passed:

1. **Today's view:** taken rows, cold rows with arrival texts including the collapsed case, the strip, the arrows and "Heute".
2. **Day switching and Back.**
3. **Booking:** a cold 14:00 slot with 90 min and 3 people led to `/booked/…`, showing the badge, the keybox line, the details and the mailto. A reload added the revisit line, and the mobile is stored as E.164.
4. **Stale form in a second tab:** it landed on the day with the focused conflict message, and the query was cleaned.
5. **Language switch on the form** kept `?d=&p=` and the contact prefill.
6. **Content pages** in DE and EN, the footer contact, and safe redirects for the `/lang` route.
7. **Without JavaScript (curl):**
   - booking gives a 303, and a resubmit gives the same id;
   - invalid fields give a 400 with the summary;
   - `cache-control: private, no-store` is set.
8. **Season states** before and after the season (20 Dec and 1 May).

**Not exercised:**
- the `changed=1` path live (it is covered by route tests);
- the 15 s slow line;
- screen-reader output beyond accessible names;
- dark mode at 360 px beyond one screenshot.

**Observations:** the favicon 404 (F2), past bookings in future tense (F10), no free hot row in the fixtures (F14), and the email address wrapping at its hyphen at 360 px (cosmetic, accepted).
